Delete your account and data

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In the app

  • Open Zumi and sign in.
  • Go to Profile → Settings → Delete my account.
  • Confirm. Your profile, listings, photos and chats are deleted within 30 days.

By email

If you cannot sign in, write to support@zumihome.com from the email of your account and ask us to delete it. We answer within 7 days.

Signed in with Google or Facebook?

Deleting your Zumi account removes the data Zumi got from Google or Facebook (your name, email and photo). You can also remove Zumi from the apps connected to your Google or Facebook account.
