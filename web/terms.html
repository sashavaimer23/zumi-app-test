1. About Zumi

Zumi is an online platform where property owners and estate agents in Zambia publish listings for sale and rent, and where people looking for a home can find them and get in touch. Zumi is not an estate agent, broker, landlord, seller or legal adviser, and is not a party to any deal between users. Zumi does not own, inspect, value or manage any property and does not hold or handle money between users. Any viewing, agreement, payment or lease is arranged directly between you and the other person, at your own decision and risk.

2. Your account

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. Give your real name and keep your contact details up to date. One account is for one person or agency. Keep your password private — you are responsible for what happens in your account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

3. Listings

  • List only property that exists and that you have the right to offer — as the owner, or with the owner's permission.
  • Estate agents must be registered with the Zambia Institute of Estate Agents (ZIEA), as required by the Estate Agents Act No. 21 of 2000.
  • Price, photos, location and details must be true and current. Use your own photos.
  • Do not post the same property twice. Mark a property as sold or rented when it is no longer available.
  • Free listings: owners — 2 active listings; agents — 5, or 10 with a checked ZIEA licence.
  • To publish a listing you must confirm your phone number with a code (once).
  • Put the pin on the map where the property really is. The address and "what's nearby" are optional, but must be true if you add them.
  • 30 days after you last confirmed a listing, Zumi asks you "Is it still available?". If you do not answer within 7 days, the listing is hidden from search (not deleted) until you confirm it. You can hide or show your listings yourself at any time. The Zumi team may hide listings that break these rules — they then go back for review before they are shown again.

4. Safety rules

It is not allowed to:

  • ask for a "viewing fee", "booking fee", "registration fee" or any other payment for showing a property — at any time: before, during or after a viewing;
  • ask for a deposit or rent before the person has seen the property and a written agreement is signed;
  • publish fake listings or pretend to be someone else;
  • refuse people because of tribe, religion, gender, disability or nationality;
  • send spam, insults or threats, or share other people's personal data;
  • use Zumi for anything illegal.

Never pay before you have seen the property in person, checked who the owner is and signed a written agreement. If anyone asks you for a viewing fee, do not pay and report the listing in Zumi.

Viewings: meet in daylight, tell someone where you are going, go with a friend if you can, and do not carry large amounts of cash. You go to viewings and meet other users at your own risk.

"Licensed agent" means the Zumi team checked the agent's registration number with the Zambia Institute of Estate Agents (ZIEA) at the time of the check. Zumi does not check identity documents, ownership documents or the property itself. Badges do not guarantee the person, the property, the price or the outcome of a deal. Always view the property and check the documents yourself.
